# Research Analyst Worklist

Status: sponsor-editable adoption output

## Role View

The product already has an initial market comparison, but ongoing research
should feed role packs, connector choices, enterprise deployment priorities,
and commercial packaging. Research should produce concise evidence, not broad
lists of tools.

## Outstanding Work

- Maintain competitor watch for LangGraph/LangSmith, CrewAI/AMP, OpenAI Agents
  SDK, Microsoft Agent Framework, Semantic Kernel, Dapr Agents, and enterprise
  agent platforms.
- Research enterprise collaboration workflows: Teams channel patterns, Slack
  equivalents, approvals, mentions, DMs, and audit expectations.
- Research stock flow catalogs beyond software delivery: architecture review,
  security review, procurement, data governance, incident response, change
  advisory, and support operations.
- Research customer evidence needs for regulated agent work: retention,
  auditability, traceability, human gates, and explainability.
- Research deployment preferences for target customers: Azure Container Apps,
  AKS, generic Kubernetes, AWS ECS/EKS, and private network constraints.
- Research license and dependency risks before adding new runtime libraries.
- Compare commercial packaging models: support subscription, enterprise UI,
  managed deployment, policy packs, and professional services.

## Risks And Decisions

- Risk: Research becomes disconnected from backlog. Mitigation: every research
  note should include product implications and candidate acceptance criteria.
- Risk: Competitor claims go stale. Mitigation: date research and cite sources.
- Decision needed: first two non-software role networks worth validating with
  design partners.

## Suggested Acceptance Criteria

- Research outputs are short, dated, cited, and tied to backlog decisions.
- Product and architecture decisions reference research where market or
  platform assumptions matter.
- Stock flow recommendations include target buyer, artifacts, gates, and
  commercial relevance.
