# Code Of Conduct

Agentic Mesh should be a professional, useful, and welcoming project for people
building open, enterprise-ready agent infrastructure.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, while still being clear about risks and trade-offs.
- Critique ideas, designs, and code, not people.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Welcome contributors with different backgrounds, employers, and levels of
  experience.
- Credit prior work and inspirations.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or abusive language.
- Discrimination or derogatory comments related to identity, background, or
  affiliation.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Doxxing or publishing private information.
- Repeated disruption after maintainers ask for a change in behavior.
- Using project spaces to promote spam, scams, or unrelated commercial offers.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, moderate
discussions, or block participants when needed to protect the project.

Enforcement should be proportionate and focused on keeping the community safe,
useful, and technically productive.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact route
published in the repository profile or project website.

If no private route is available yet, open a public issue asking for a private
maintainer contact without including sensitive personal details.
