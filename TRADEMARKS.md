# Trademark Guidelines

The Apache License 2.0 grants rights to use, copy, modify, and distribute the
software. It does not grant trademark rights.

The names "Agentic Mesh" and "Agentic Mesh" logos or marks, if any, are reserved
for identifying the project and official offerings.

## You May

- Refer to the project as Agentic Mesh when describing compatibility,
  dependency, integration, or origin.
- State that your project uses, extends, integrates with, or is compatible with
  Agentic Mesh, if true.
- Use the project name in package metadata, documentation, blog posts, talks,
  and issue reports in a truthful and non-misleading way.

## You May Not

- Claim endorsement, certification, partnership, or official status without
  written permission.
- Use the Agentic Mesh name or marks for a competing product or hosted service
  in a way that could confuse users about origin or control.
- Use modified logos or marks in a way that implies they are official.
- Register domains, packages, social accounts, or company names that imply
  official ownership of Agentic Mesh without permission.

## Forks And Distributions

You may fork and redistribute the software under the Apache License 2.0.

If your fork materially changes behavior, security posture, compatibility, or
support expectations, make that clear to users and do not present it as an
official Agentic Mesh release.

## Commercial Offerings

Commercial support, hosted services, managed deployments, training, and
enterprise add-ons may use separate marks, terms, and agreements.

These guidelines may be updated as the project establishes a formal legal
entity or trademark owner.
