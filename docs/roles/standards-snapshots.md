# Standards Snapshots For Role Charters

Agentic Mesh role charters use established standards as source material, not as
runtime dependencies or rigid process lock-in. Runtime prompts must cite these
curated references from the repository instead of fetching live standards pages.

## Prompt And Workflow Shape

- BMAD Method agents: specialist agents, workflow triggers, artifact-driven
  handoffs, and role-specific operating modes.
  Source: https://docs.bmad-method.org/reference/agents/
- Prompt engineering practice: clear task instructions, context separation,
  examples, tool-use guidance, evaluation scenarios, and regression-driven
  prompt improvement.
  Source: https://platform.openai.com/docs/guides/prompt-engineering

## Digital Role Expectations

- UK Government Digital and Data Capability Framework: current role and skill
  expectations for public-sector digital delivery roles.
  Source: https://ddat-capability-framework.service.gov.uk/
- Product manager: product quality, value, prioritisation, outcome measurement,
  and product lifecycle leadership.
  Source: https://ddat-capability-framework.service.gov.uk/role/product-manager
- Business analyst: business problem analysis, options, user and business needs,
  and success measures.
  Source: https://ddat-capability-framework.service.gov.uk/role/business-analyst
- Delivery manager: agile delivery management, team flow, delivery risks, and
  outcome tracking.
  Source: https://ddat-capability-framework.service.gov.uk/role/delivery-manager
- Enterprise architect: translating business strategy into business change and
  technical delivery across organisational levels.
  Source: https://ddat-capability-framework.service.gov.uk/role/enterprise-architect
- Solution architect: solution-level technical design, constraints, and risk.
  Source: https://ddat-capability-framework.service.gov.uk/role/solution-architect
- Technical architect: technical architecture design and build guidance.
  Source: https://ddat-capability-framework.service.gov.uk/role/technical-architect
- Prompt engineer: Agentic Mesh specialist role adapted from prompt
  engineering practice, BMAD-style agent workflow design, technical
  architecture discipline, and QA-style behavioural regression thinking.
  Source: internal Agentic Mesh role template `config/roles/prompt-engineer.yaml`
- Security architect: system or service security design, security documentation,
  and security architecture patterns.
  Source: https://ddat-capability-framework.service.gov.uk/role/security-architect
- Test engineer and quality assurance test analyst: test engineering standards,
  risk identification, test planning, execution, and defect reporting.
  Sources:
  https://ddat-capability-framework.service.gov.uk/role/test-engineer and
  https://ddat-capability-framework.service.gov.uk/role/quality-assurance-test-analyst
- Interaction designer, service designer, user researcher, and content designer:
  evidence-led user-centred design, end-to-end service journeys, research, and
  content quality.
  Sources:
  https://ddat-capability-framework.service.gov.uk/role/interaction-designer,
  https://ddat-capability-framework.service.gov.uk/role/service-designer,
  https://ddat-capability-framework.service.gov.uk/role/user-researcher, and
  https://ddat-capability-framework.service.gov.uk/role/content-designer

## Skills And Governance

- SFIA 9: focused skills-profile inspiration. Use 5 to 7 relevant skill anchors
  per role and tailor them to Agentic Mesh context.
  Source: https://sfia-online.org/en/tools-and-resources/standard-industry-skills-profiles/sfia-9-skills-for-role-families-job-titles
- Scrum Guide: product accountability, transparency, adaptation, and delivery
  collaboration boundaries.
  Source: https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html
- TOGAF: enterprise architecture governance, architecture content, viewpoints,
  and traceability.
  Source: https://www.opengroup.org/togaf

## Quality And Security

- ISTQB and BDD practice: test planning, defect evidence, risk-based testing,
  and behaviour scenarios.
  Sources: https://istqb.org/ and https://cucumber.io/docs
- OWASP SAMM: secure software maturity across governance, design,
  implementation, verification, and operations.
  Source: https://owasp.org/www-project-samm/
- NIST SSDF: prepare the organisation, protect software, produce well-secured
  software, and respond to vulnerabilities.
  Source: https://csrc.nist.gov/projects/ssdf
