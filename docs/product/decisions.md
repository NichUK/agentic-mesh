# Product Decisions

Owner role: Product Manager

This document records durable product decisions that apply beyond one slice.
Work-item-specific product definitions and sign-offs belong in the work-item
dossier and should link here when they create reusable product direction.

## Current Decisions

- V3 should prioritise human-team style collaboration with specialist role
  agents, not runtime-owned lifecycle choreography.
- Messaging inputs are conversational by default; dedicated work systems may
  create queue items directly.
