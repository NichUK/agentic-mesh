# Business Briefs

Owner role: Business Analyst

This document is the durable home for business-context briefs that apply
across work items. Slice-specific business analysis belongs in
`/documents/work-items/{work_item_id}/index.md` and linked artifacts.

Business briefs should capture stakeholder intent, problem framing, affected
capabilities, constraints, assumptions, open questions, and acceptance-relevant
business outcomes. They should avoid prescribing technical solutions unless a
stakeholder has explicitly made that decision.
