# Business Research Notes

Owner role: Business Analyst

Use this document for durable business research notes that support stakeholder
needs, operating context, capability fit, and enterprise adoption assumptions.
Research that becomes a decision should be linked from the decision register
or relevant work-item dossier.

## Evidence Standard

- Record source, date, and relevance.
- Distinguish observed fact from role interpretation.
- Link conclusions to affected requirements, risks, or open questions.
